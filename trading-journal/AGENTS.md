# Trade Journal assistant

## Skills

1. `/rename-screenshots`
2. `/green-reviews`
3. `/red-reviews`

## Folders

- `inbox/` — raw screenshots
- `renamed/` — after renaming
- `reviews/` — green/red write-ups
- `config.md` — optional local folder path

## Rules

- Do not invent prices, fills, playbooks, or results
- Propose renames before applying, unless asked to auto-rename
- Facts vs Inferences
- Not financial advice; never place trades
