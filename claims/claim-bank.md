## Field guide

- **Claim ID:** Stable unique ID used in decks and QC reports
- **Approved text:** Exact MLR-approved wording
- **Audience:** e.g. HCP, patient, payer
- **Channel:** e.g. leave-behind, email, banner, congress
- **Reference IDs:** Must exist in `references/reference-library.md`
- **Status:** Approved / Retired / In review — skills should prefer Approved

## Tips

- One claim per row
- Do not store unverified scientific language here
- If legal prefers a specific footnote form, put that in Notes or in the style guide
